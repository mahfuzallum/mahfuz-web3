Step 1

Sources

Profiles whose newest followers GrowX follows. Their latest followers are active right now and already follow accounts like yours - the best audience there is.

Pick sources like a pro: active accounts in your exact niche with 5K-500K followers whose audience is real people (creators, communities, niche news, competitors). Add 5-15 sources - GrowX rotates between them and ranks each by its real follow-back rate. Avoid celebrities and big brands: their followers rarely follow back.

Or follow these exact accountsPaste usernames or profile links - as many as you like. Anyone you already follow is skipped; the rest join the Follow queue and are followed with your pacing, your daily number and every safety setting.

How GrowX works
Four modes, one engine - then it runs by itself.

GrowX works through your own signed-in x.com session. No passwords, no API keys, nothing sent anywhere else.

Questions

            
Does it keep running when I close the popup or the dashboard?
Yes. The engine lives in the extension's background worker and continues until you press Stop. It survives tab switches, X tab reloads, worker restarts and - unless you turn it off in Settings - Chrome restarts. Keep Chrome open; if it's closed, GrowX continues when you open it again.

            
What are the four modes: Follow, Unfollow, Follow back, Comment?
Only one runs at a time, and one click switches it (on the Overview or on Autopilot & Safety). Follow follows new people from your sources. Unfollow cleans out non-followers - oldest following first, or newest first if you pick that in U1. Follow back follows the people who follow you but you don't follow yet - newest follower first - until nobody is left. Each mode has its own pace, its own random breaks, its own hours, its own daily number and its own "when X refuses" plan (see below). Comment writes AI replies on the posts you choose and - if you let it - posts them. Follow and Follow back share X's follow limit; Comment has its own, because X counts replies separately. You can also have GrowX work through the modes one after another by itself - see the next question.

            
Can GrowX work through all three modes by itself, one after another?
Yes - that is Mode sequence, the card at the top of Autopilot & Safety. Switch it on and arrange the three mode cards in the order you want with the arrows; the switch on a card leaves that mode out of the sequence without losing its place. Each card has two numbers of its own: Target per turn (how many it does before handing over) and Target per day (its ceiling for the whole day). When a mode reaches its turn target, GrowX stops it, takes a random transition break inside the range you set, and only once that break has finished does the next mode start. Leave a turn target at 0 and that mode does its whole day in one go. With Repeat the sequence on, the list goes round again, so a day can be several rounds of follow → follow back → unfollow. A mode that is finished for today, or that X has limited today, is passed over, and the Live preview on the right shows exactly how it will run. Pick a mode by hand at any time and your choice wins: the waiting transition is dropped. With switching off, GrowX behaves exactly as before and stays in the one mode you picked.

            
Are the breaks random? Can each mode have its own?
Yes to both. Every mode has two ranges of its own, and a break is always a random value inside them - never outside: how many actions before a break (at least to at most), and how long the break lasts (at least to at most). Follow sets them in section 3, Follow back in B4 and Unfollow in U3, and none of them affects the others. Put the same number in both boxes and you get exactly that number, which is what older versions did - so nothing changes after an update until you widen a range yourself. Follow's extra break rules (section 4) have their own range too, and the transition break between two modes has its own range in the sequence card.

            
Who unfollowed me, and can I unfollow them back?
Open Unfollowed you in the sidebar. GrowX notices people who followed you and later stopped during the checks it already makes - the full follower scan, the follow-back checks and the look-up right before a follow-back or an unfollow - so finding them costs no extra requests to X. The page shows how many were found today, the whole list with the user ids kept, and which of them you can still unfollow (you follow them, they are not on your Never unfollow list, and GrowX has not already unfollowed them). Nobody here is unfollowed automatically. Tick one, several, or every eligible one at once, and they go to the front of the Unfollow mode queue - still with the re-check right before, your Never unfollow list, your daily unfollow number and your usual delays and breaks.

            
What happens when X refuses a follow (or a follow fails)?
GrowX protects the account in 4 steps, and you write the time of each step yourself (Autopilot & Safety: section 5 for Follow, B8 for Follow back, U8 for Unfollow). Step 1: the first refusal or failed follow - an 18 minute break. Step 2: if X refuses again right after those 18 minutes - a break of 30 to 40 minutes. Step 3: if X refuses again - 3 to 4 hours. Step 4: if X refuses once more - GrowX stops for the rest of the day and starts again by itself the next day (keep "Turn ON by itself every day" on). Steps 1 to 3 have an on/off switch; step 4 is always on because it is what keeps the account safe. The steps count refusals in a row: as soon as a follow works again, the plan starts again from step 1. Follow and Follow back share the steps (X counts their follows together); Unfollow has its own. A break that is running is never skipped, not even if you press Stop and Start.

            
How do I set when GrowX turns ON and OFF each day?
Each mode has a Daily ON / OFF time card on the Autopilot & Safety page (section 6 for Follow, U4 for Unfollow, B5 for Follow back). Write the time it should turn ON and the time it should turn OFF, and pick the days. With Turn ON by itself every day switched on, GrowX starts by itself at the ON time - no clicking - and goes to sleep at the OFF time. The bar shows the working hours of the day and where "now" is. It also turns off earlier once the day's number is done, and carries on at the next ON time if it isn't.

            
Can Unfollow start with the newest accounts I follow?
Yes. In U1. How many unfollows, set Who first to Newest following first. The default is Oldest following first. It applies to the whole list, the inactive check and "active followers" too. If you change it while a list is half done, the rest of the list is turned around - nobody is unfollowed twice and nobody is lost. Follow back has the same choice for your followers (B1), and Follow can pick the best match, the newest found or the oldest found person in your queue first (section 1).

            
What does "Per hour: Auto" mean?
You do not have to write the hourly number any more. With Auto on, GrowX works it out for the mode you use: from your number for the day, your active hours, your delays and your breaks - just above the busiest hour your own delays and breaks can make, so it never cuts into your rhythm (for example 15 follows, then 18 minutes), and never above X's safe maximum. Change the day's number, a delay or a break and the hourly number follows by itself. Switch Auto off if you want to write the number yourself.

            
How does Follow back choose who to follow?
It reads who follows you and who you follow, and keeps the followers you don't follow yet. It starts with your most recent follower and works towards the oldest. Right before following back, every batch of 100 is checked again: still following you, not already followed, not private, and (if you switch it on in B6) has a verified badge. Just before each follow-back GrowX also counts the follower's posts - an account with fewer than 5 posts, replies and reposts in the last 60 days is treated as inactive and skipped (you can change both numbers in B7). If X doesn't show something, the follower is skipped, never guessed. It finishes your daily number first. If fewer people are waiting than your number, it follows all of them, rests, and looks for new followers again later.

            
Can Follow back follow back only verified accounts?
Yes. Switch on B6. Verified accounts only on the Autopilot & Safety page. Only followers with a verified badge (blue, gold or grey check) are followed back; everyone else is skipped and looked at again after 3 days. Most followers are not verified, so far fewer than your daily number may qualify, and GrowX looks through your whole follower list to find them. GrowX reads the badge from X right before; if X doesn't show it, the follower is skipped (never guessed) and a warning appears in the Overview's Activity box (Events tab).

            
What is Comment mode, and does GrowX post the replies itself?
Comment mode is the fourth mode. You give it posts - pasted links, accounts on a watch list, target accounts you also want to follow, and the replies people leave on your own posts - and for each one GrowX reads the post on X, asks your AI model for one reply with your prompt, checks that reply against your rules in GrowX (banned words and every form of them, length, lines, lowercase, no emoji, no hashtags, no links, not a copy of the post, not like your last replies), and only then posts it. You choose what happens at the end, in C1 → Publishing: GrowX posts it sends the reply through your own signed-in x.com session, waits, and reads the post back before the row is marked Posted - nothing is ever counted on a guess; or I post it myself, where GrowX only writes and keeps the drafts ready for you. Either way it keeps your pace: a random wait between replies, a break every few replies, a per-hour number, a daily goal, its own ON / OFF hours and its own rest day.

            
Does Comment mode cost money? How much?
GrowX itself is free. The writing is done by your own OpenAI key, which you paste in C4 and which never leaves this browser. The model table shows what 1,000 replies cost with each model, and the Comment page shows what today really cost, worked out from the token counts OpenAI returns - never from an estimate. Set a daily spend cap in C10 and GrowX stops writing for the day when it reaches it, with a notification. gpt-4.1-mini is the sensible default: it follows long rule lists well and a day of replies costs a few cents.

            
Can it reply to the same post twice?
No. Every post id you have ever replied to is kept forever, and a link in any form - x.com, twitter.com, mobile, /i/web/status/, with ?s=20 or /photo/1 - comes down to the same id. GrowX also reads the post on X right before writing, so a reply you made by hand or on another device counts too. Both checks are always on and cannot be switched off.

            
What does Comment mode skip?
Posts you already replied to, your own posts, posts older than your limit (7 days by default), posts where the author has limited who may reply, posts that are deleted or not visible to you, posts by an author who already had their reply today, and posts the model itself calls off topic (it answers SKIP and GrowX moves on instead of forcing something). Every skip is logged with its reason on the Comment page.

            
Can Comment mode take its turn in the mode sequence?
Yes. Comment has its own row in the Mode sequence card and starts switched off there, so nothing changes until you want it to. Switch it on and it takes its turn like the others: it does its Target per turn (C1), then the transition break runs, then the next mode starts. Comment does not use X's follow limit, so it fits anywhere in the order. Leave Don't run with the other modes at the same time on (C3) and Comment waits until Follow, Follow back and Unfollow have had their turn.

            
How do the waits in Comment mode work?
Three of them, each a random time inside a range you set in C2, because that is what a person actually does: after the post opens there is a pause before writing; the reply is typed; then a pause of a second or two before it is sent. With Like the post before replying on there is a fourth - the gap between the like and the reply. On top of that is the normal wait between replies and the break every few replies. If X ever refuses the direct request, GrowX falls back to the post page and does it the way you would: open the reply box, type, wait, press Reply.

            
Can I get run updates on my phone?
Yes - Telegram, in C11, for all four modes. Make a bot with @BotFather, paste the token and your chat id, and pick which events you want: started, paused, resumed, finished, X warnings, failures in a row, breaks, drafts ready, a watched account posting, and a daily report at the time you choose. The token stays in this browser and is never written into a backup.

            
What happens to my comment history?
It only grows. Every day's totals and every post id are kept forever in this browser, and a backup file is written to Downloads/GrowX/ once a day. A restore merges: it can raise a total, never lower one. The Comment history page shows the all-time number, the day-by-day table with a running total, and the backup buttons.

            
Do I need to keep an X tab open?
No. GrowX uses any open x.com tab, or opens a small pinned one by itself. You can keep browsing X normally.

            
How many followers can I expect?
Typical follow-back rates with good sources are 15-35%. At 300 follows a day and a 25% rate that's ~75 new followers a day, ~2,250 a month. The Analytics planner shows your real numbers. 5,000+ a month needs X Premium (higher limits) or a very well-targeted niche.

            
What about X's 5,000 following cap?
X lets you follow 5,000 accounts; beyond that the cap grows with your followers. GrowX pauses at the cap and waits. Switch GrowX to Unfollow mode to clear out people who didn't follow back, and GrowX carries on by itself once there is room again.

            
Why did GrowX pause or stop?
It always tells you on the Overview. Breaks, hourly and daily caps and active hours are normal. A "safety rest" means X showed a warning and GrowX backed off. It stops completely only when X needs you: signed out, a verification check, or two "automated" warnings in two days. If X keeps refusing, GrowX takes the breaks of the 3-step plan and then stops for the rest of the day.

            
Is it safe?
No tool can promise zero risk - X decides. GrowX is built to look and behave like a careful person: it uses X's own web requests, randomises everything, respects every limit X signals, and never clicks through a challenge. Start with the Safe or Balanced pace, use active hours, and turn on warm-up for new accounts.

            
Where is my data?
Only in this browser (chrome.storage). GrowX has no servers, no analytics and no tracking. Requests go to x.com and X's own scripts on twimg.com - and, only if you switch Comment mode on and add your own key, to api.openai.com to write a reply and api.telegram.org to send your own updates. Nothing goes to Unique Labs.

            
Emergency stop?
Press Alt + Shift + S anywhere in Chrome.

          

          

            
Safety playbook

            
    
              
  • New account? Turn on Warm-up and use the Safe pace for the first week.
    • 
              
  • Keep a Daily ON / OFF time - nobody follows people 24 hours a day.
    • 
              
  • Stay under 400 follows a day on a normal account.
    • 
              
  • Post and reply now and then: active accounts get more follow-backs and less scrutiny.
    • 
              
  • Unfollow non-followers slowly, after 7-14 days - never on the same day as heavy following. With automatic switching on, the transition break keeps following and unfollowing apart.
    • 
              
  • Check Unfollowed you now and then, but unfollow people back in small batches, not all at once.
    • 
              
  • If GrowX reports a warning, let it rest. Don't restart it at full speed.
    • 
              
  • In Comment mode, keep one reply per author per day and start at the Safe pace - replies are far more visible to other people than follows, and reports come from people, not from X.
    • 
              
  • Read your first twenty replies before letting Comment mode run on its own. If any of them sound like a bot, that is a prompt problem, not a pace problem.
